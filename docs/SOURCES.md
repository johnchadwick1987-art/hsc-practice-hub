## Alignment decisions

The HSC syllabus identifies Industry Study, Major Project (Design, Management and Communication; Production), and Industry Related Manufacturing Technology. Our ten categories are revision groupings across these areas rather than official syllabus headings. Topic percentages in the dashboard are practice results, not HSC assessment weightings.

HSC Multimedia content includes text and typography, bitmap/vector graphics, resolution and formats, audio sampling and file size, video compression and editing, animation, World Wide Web delivery, software and hardware requirements, intellectual property, and WHS. Industry Study covers business structure, technical and personnel factors, WHS, environmental and sociological considerations. Questions about project planning and evaluation also reinforce Major Project knowledge; do not infer direct written-paper coverage for every project-work question.

The simulation selects Multimedia-related manufacturing content for Sections I/II and Industry Study for Section III. It is a small representative sample, not a complete syllabus test. It omits many specific topics such as detailed typography, analogue/digital comparisons, motion capture and several animation techniques. Add reviewed questions to improve breadth.

Some questions use contemporary examples (SSD storage, cloud collaboration, accessibility) to apply underlying principles. They are not claimed to be exact syllabus wording. Only checked broad references are saved in question metadata. Teachers should continue to align vocabulary, depth and criteria with current official material.

## Content review

Mark bands are original practice guidance, not NESA-approved guidelines. Model responses illustrate an acceptable approach; credit relevant accurate alternatives. Teacher review is still appropriate before a formal classroom mock. No source year is assigned to any original question.
